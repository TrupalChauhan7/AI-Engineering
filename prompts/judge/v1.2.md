# Faithfulness Judge Prompt — v1.2 (Selene-Mini, absolute rubric)

> For the purpose-built evaluator **Atla Selene-Mini** (Llama-3.1-8B family —
> different family from the MedGemma generator, satisfying the self-enhancement
> rule). Selene is TRAINED on this absolute-scoring format (reasoning + a 1–5
> rubric score), so we use it that way instead of the raw JSON prompts that
> failed on general llama-8b (v1.1). SOURCE-GROUNDED: the note is judged against
> the TRANSCRIPT only — no gold reference note is shown (fair-fight + no leak).
> Parsed as `**Result:** N`; mapped to faithfulness (N−1)/4 ∈ [0,1].
> Keep v1.0 and v1.1 unchanged.

## User
You are tasked with evaluating a response based on a given instruction (which may contain an Input) and a scoring rubric that serve as the evaluation standard. Provide a comprehensive feedback on the response quality strictly adhering to the scoring rubric, followed by an integer score between 1 and 5. Avoid generating any additional opening, closing, or explanations.

Here are some rules of the evaluation:
(1) You should prioritize evaluating whether the response satisfies the provided rubric. The basis of your score should depend exactly on the rubric. However, the response does not need to explicitly address points raised in the rubric. Rather, evaluate the response based on the criteria outlined in the rubric.

Your reply should strictly follow this format:
**Reasoning:** <Your feedback>

**Result:** <an integer between 1 and 5>

Here is the data:

Instruction:
```
Assess the FAITHFULNESS of a clinical SOAP note to the consultation transcript it was written from. A faithful note (a) states only information supported by the transcript — no invented findings, medications, history, values, or follow-up (hallucinations); and (b) captures the clinically important facts from the transcript — no critical omissions. Judge support against the transcript ONLY; do not rely on outside medical knowledge.

Consultation transcript:
{{transcript}}
```

Response:
```
{{note}}
```

Score Rubrics:
[Is the clinical note faithful to the consultation transcript — free of hallucinations and free of clinically important omissions?]
Score 1: Severely unfaithful — multiple hallucinations and/or critical omissions; the note misrepresents the consultation.
Score 2: Substantially unfaithful — several unsupported statements and/or important omissions that materially affect reliability.
Score 3: Moderately faithful — mostly supported, but with some unsupported details or notable omissions.
Score 4: Largely faithful — well supported by the transcript, with only minor omissions or negligible unsupported detail.
Score 5: Fully faithful — every statement is supported by the transcript and all clinically important facts are captured.
